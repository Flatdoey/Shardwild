# Shardwild

A pixel bullet-hell realm crawler for your phone. Death is permanent. Fame is the score you take with you.

## How to play

Choose a hero — Pyromancer, Strider, or Bulwark — or play one you already saved. This device keeps four heroes, and two of the same class are fine. Each hero has their own level, stats, equipment, and backpack. You start in the Haven, a safe hub. Walk into the purple portal (the quest arrow points at it) to enter the Shardwild. The teal Vault portal on the right opens a shared chest room; those chests follow every hero.

The realm gets harder from the shore toward the center: Saltmarsh Shore, Greenmarch, Ashen Wilds, then the Godlands, where Morvane, the Hollow King, rises among the gods. Monsters stay in the region they spawned in. They may chase a few tiles past the border, then they walk home. Kill them for experience (level 20 is the cap), take loot from bags, and drink stat elixirs. Each class has its own maximum for every stat. An elixir raises that stat until the cap; at the cap it says maxed and the bottle stays in your pack. The Haven test chest holds a Potion of Mastery that fills every stat to the cap. It never drops in the realm. Gear runs from plain T0–T2 through trimmed T3–T5 and ornate T6–T7. Boss-only untiered (UT) pieces have their own shape and a shimmer in the bag, backpack, and test chest. When you die, that hero is gone and the run's fame is recorded. Your best runs stay in the Hall of Fame on this device.

## Controls

Shardwild is landscape-only. Hold the phone sideways. In portrait the game pauses and asks you to rotate.

**Touch**

- The realm stays on the left. Stats, gear, and your backpack stay open on the right.
- Drag the bottom-left of the playfield to move.
- Drag the lower-right of the playfield, just left of the inventory, to aim. Your weapon fires while you hold.
- The ability button, left of the aim stick, spends MP (Ember Nova, Thorn Volley, or Aegis Slam).
- ⌂ Haven sits above the aim stick. It is a large button on the playfield, just left of the inventory, and it returns you to the Haven. Tapping it does not fire.
- AUTO toggles auto-aim. Heroes opens character select, where you can play, create, or delete a hero. Deleting asks you to confirm.
- Drink healing draughts, mana draughts, and stat elixirs from the backpack. Tap the slot. The character panel shows each stat against its class cap, for example ATT 45/75, and a maxed stat is gold.
- The Vault portal is on the right side of the Haven. Walk onto it, or tap Enter when you are close. The room has four chests of eight slots and a gold exit back to the Haven. Nothing in there will attack you. A chest opens when you stand next to it; tap or drag items between it and your backpack, the same way as a loot bag.
- The speaker under the minimap cycles sound: effects and music, effects only, then muted. The choice is saved on this device. Music is a soft loop — calm in the Haven, tenser in the realm, harder in a boss fight.
- Stand on a loot bag and it opens under your backpack. Tap an item to take it; a clear upgrade equips itself.
- The Haven's test chest (left of the spawn) lists every item. Tabs filter by class, arrows page the list, and taking an item copies it. Items your class can't use are dimmed. The Test tab holds one Godslayer weapon per class; each one kills any enemy, including bosses, in a single hit, and none of them drop in the realm. Unlock lights every beacon.
- Tap the minimap to open a map of the whole realm. Tap outside it or Close to dismiss it. The realm pauses while the map is open.
- Walk up to a glowing beacon to unlock it. On the map, lit beacons warp you there. Locked beacons stay grey. A Beacon Key in the test chest does the same as Unlock.
- Tap a potion or elixir in the backpack to drink it. Tap other gear to equip it, or to drop it into an open bag.
- Drag items between the bag, backpack, and equipment. Hold a finger on an item to read it.

**Keyboard and mouse**

- WASD moves. Hold the mouse button to aim and fire.
- Space uses your ability.
- Q or 1 drinks a healing draught. E or 2 drinks a mana draught.
- F takes the first item from a nearby bag.
- R or H returns to the Haven. T toggles auto-aim. M opens the map. Escape closes it.
- On the title screen, 1 / 2 / 3 picks a class when you are creating a hero, and plays that slot when you are choosing one. Enter plays the active hero.

Open `index.html` to play. On GitHub Pages the game is served from `/Shardwild/`.
