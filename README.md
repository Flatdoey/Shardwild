# Shardwild

A pixel bullet-hell realm crawler for your phone. Death is permanent. Fame is the score you take with you.

## How to play

Choose a hero — Pyromancer, Strider, or Bulwark — or continue a saved run. You start in the Haven, a safe hub. Walk into the portal (the quest arrow points at it) to enter the Shardwild.

The realm gets harder from the shore toward the center: Saltmarsh Shore, Greenmarch, Ashen Wilds, then Starfall Heights, where Morvane, the Hollow King, rises. Kill monsters for experience (level 20 is the cap), take loot from bags, and drink stat elixirs. When you die, that hero is gone and the run's fame is recorded. Your best runs stay in the Hall of Fame on this device.

## Controls

**Touch**

- Drag on the left to move.
- Drag on the right to aim. Your weapon fires while you hold.
- The ability button spends MP (Ember Nova, Thorn Volley, or Aegis Slam).
- HP and MP drink a draught.
- ⌂ returns to the Haven. ≡ opens your inventory. AUTO toggles auto-aim.
- Tap items in a loot bag to take them.

**Keyboard and mouse**

- WASD moves. Hold the mouse button to aim and fire.
- Space uses your ability.
- Q or 1 drinks a healing draught. E or 2 drinks a mana draught.
- I or B opens the inventory. F takes the first item from a nearby bag.
- R or H returns to the Haven. T toggles auto-aim.
- On the title screen, 1 / 2 / 3 picks a hero and Enter continues a save.

Open `index.html` to play. On GitHub Pages the game is served from `/Shardwild/`.
