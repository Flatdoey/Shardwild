# Shardwild

A pixel bullet-hell realm crawler for your phone. Death is permanent. Fame is the score you take with you.

## How to play

Choose a hero — Pyromancer, Strider, or Bulwark — or continue a saved run. You start in the Haven, a safe hub. Walk into the portal (the quest arrow points at it) to enter the Shardwild.

The realm gets harder from the shore toward the center: Saltmarsh Shore, Greenmarch, Ashen Wilds, then Starfall Heights, where Morvane, the Hollow King, rises. Kill monsters for experience (level 20 is the cap), take loot from bags, and drink stat elixirs. When you die, that hero is gone and the run's fame is recorded. Your best runs stay in the Hall of Fame on this device.

## Controls

Shardwild is landscape-only. Hold the phone sideways. In portrait the game pauses and asks you to rotate.

**Touch**

- The realm stays on the left. Stats, gear, and your backpack stay open on the right.
- Drag the bottom-left of the playfield to move.
- Drag the lower-right of the playfield to aim. Your weapon fires while you hold.
- The ability button spends MP (Ember Nova, Thorn Volley, or Aegis Slam).
- HP and MP drink a draught.
- ⌂ returns to the Haven. AUTO toggles auto-aim.
- Stand on a loot bag and it opens under your backpack. Tap an item to take it; a clear upgrade equips itself.
- The Haven's test chest (left of the spawn) lists every item. Tabs filter by class, arrows page the list, and taking an item copies it. Items your class can't use are dimmed. Unlock lights every beacon.
- Tap the minimap to open a map of the whole realm. Tap outside it or Close to dismiss it. The realm pauses while the map is open.
- Walk up to a glowing beacon to unlock it. On the map, lit beacons warp you there. Locked beacons stay grey. A Beacon Key in the test chest does the same as Unlock.
- Tap a potion or elixir in the backpack to drink it. Tap other gear to equip it, or to drop it into an open bag.
- Drag items between the bag, backpack, and equipment. Hold a finger on an item to read it.

**Keyboard and mouse**

- WASD moves. Hold the mouse button to aim and fire.
- Space uses your ability.
- Q or 1 drinks a healing draught. E or 2 drinks a mana draught.
- F takes the first item from a nearby bag.
- R or H returns to the Haven. T toggles auto-aim. M opens the map. Escape closes it.
- On the title screen, 1 / 2 / 3 picks a hero and Enter continues a save.

Open `index.html` to play. On GitHub Pages the game is served from `/Shardwild/`.
